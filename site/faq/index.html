Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Turbo Keysmith’s mobile locksmith service in the OKC metro.

Are you a mobile locksmith?

Yes. Turbo Keysmith is fully mobile — we come to you anywhere in the Oklahoma City metro, whether you’re at home, at work, or stranded in a parking lot.

What are your hours?

We’re open 24 hours a day, Monday through Saturday, for emergencies, and on Sunday until 5:00 am. Call or text 405-870-5397 any time.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Oklahoma City, Edmond, Moore, Norman, Yukon, Warr Acres, Bethany, Midwest City, Del City, and the surrounding metro.

Can you make a car key if I lost all of mine?

Yes. As a NASTF-authorized locksmith with dealer-level tools, we can cut and program replacement keys and fobs for most makes and models on-site — usually faster and for less than the dealership.

How can I pay?

We accept cash and credit cards. You can also pay your invoice securely online on our Pay Now page.

Are you licensed?

Yes — Turbo Keysmith is a licensed Oklahoma locksmith, OK Lic. #AC441081.
