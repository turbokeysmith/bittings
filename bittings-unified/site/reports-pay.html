06 · Numbers

Reports and pay.

See what the shop sold, what it kept, and what is owed in sales tax and pay.

What you can do.

  • Sales with charts.By day, week, month, quarter or year.
  • Real profit.Profit after the cost of the key, card fees and contractors.
  • Sales tax worksheet.Sales by category (Keys, Accessories, Services / labor), taxable, not taxed and tax-exempt, the figures for the monthly return, and a file with every line.
  • Tax-exempt sales.Kept with the certificate on file.
  • Pay.Hourly wages, commission and contractors paid, and what is left after pay.
  • Export and filter.Export to a spreadsheet, or filter by technician.
