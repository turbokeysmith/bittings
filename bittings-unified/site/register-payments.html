04 · Money

Register and card payments.

Ring up parts and services at the counter, by vehicle. Take the payment and hand over a receipt.

The register is made for the counter PC and a tablet. On a phone you can still text a pay link or send a receipt.

What you can do.

  • Ring up by vehicle.Add parts and services by VIN or year, make and model.
  • Take a card.Use a Stripe reader, type the card in, or text the customer a pay link.
  • Cash and checks.Cash asks what the customer handed over and shows the change. Checks are there for business customers.
  • Receipts.Print on the Star thermal printer with your logo, or send by email or text. The cash drawer opens.
  • Your own Stripe account.Payments go through your shop’s Stripe account, and the money goes to your bank.
  • Day closeout.Close out the day when the counter is done.
