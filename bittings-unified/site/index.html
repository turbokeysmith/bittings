Master key systems and pin charts.

For residential and commercial locksmiths. Design the system, pin the locks, and keep the whole building on file under the customer.

Questions

The ones we get asked first.

Do I have to install anything?

No. Bittings runs in the browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

What does it run on?

iPhone, Android, tablets and any PC or Mac with a current browser. Everyone in the shop sees the same jobs and the same stock.

How do card payments work?

Through Stripe, on your own Stripe account, so the money goes to your bank. You can use a card reader at the counter, type a card in, or text the customer a link to pay.

Can I bring my inventory with me?

Yes. Import your parts list from a spreadsheet, match the columns, check the preview and it is in.

Who can see what?

Each shop's data is its own. Everyone signs in, and each role sees only what it should — a technician sees prices, not what the key cost you.

Who makes it?

A working automotive locksmith shop in Oklahoma City. It is the software we run our own counter and van on, every day.
