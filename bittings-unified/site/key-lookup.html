01 · Lookup

Key lookup.

Type a VIN or pick the year, make and model. You see what the key is and which ones on your shelf will do the job.

What you can do.

  • Type a VIN.It is decoded to the year, make and model for you. Or pick year, make and model yourself.
  • See the key details.Keyway, chip or transponder system, and how it programs, for example by OBD.
  • Know if it is OEM-only.The result says whether the key is OEM-only or needs NASTF.
  • Keys from your own shelf.It lists the keys you have that fit, with your price and how many are in stock.
  • Lishi and keyway reference.Look up the pick and the keyway without leaving the screen.
  • Programmer and key-code links.Jump to programmers and key-code tools. It works the same on your phone in the field.
