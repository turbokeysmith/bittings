Terms of Service

Bittings is a tool: the key, chip and programming information in it is a reference to check, and you stay responsible for the work your shop does, for proving who owns a vehicle, and for your own licences, NASTF duties and taxes.

Bittings is shop software for locksmiths, run by Turbo Keysmith LLC, an Oklahoma company ("Bittings", "we", "us"). "You" means the business that signs up, and the person who accepts these terms for it. By creating an account, asking us to create one, or using Bittings, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use Bittings.

Bittings is for businesses. It is not for personal or household use, and you must be at least 18 to open an account.

2. Your account and your people

  • Give us accurate details about your shop and keep them current.
  • Each person gets their own login. Do not share logins or PINs.
  • You choose who in your shop has access and what role they have, and you are responsible for what they do in Bittings.
  • Owners and managers are asked to use two-step sign-in. Keep it on.
  • Tell us straight away at support@bittings.app if you think someone has got into your account.

3. Plans, prices and billing

  • Your plan and price are the ones shown on our Plans page when you signed up, or the ones we agreed with you in writing.

  • Prices do not include sales tax. If tax applies to your subscription we add it to the bill.
  • We can change prices. We will email the account owner at least 30 days before a change applies to you, and you can cancel before it does.
  • If you joined on a founder price, that price stays in place for as long as your plan stays active. If you cancel and come back later, the prices at that time apply.
  • If a payment fails we will tell you and try again. If the bill is still unpaid 14 days later we may switch the account to read-only until it is paid.

4. Card payments from your customers

  • Card payments are processed by Stripe on your own Stripe account. The money goes from Stripe to your bank. We never hold your customers' money, and we never see or store full card numbers.
  • You must accept Stripe's terms to take cards. Stripe's fees are Stripe's and are charged by Stripe.
  • On the Starter plan we add a platform fee of 1% of each card payment, collected through Stripe at the time of the payment. The Pro plan has no Bittings fee on card payments.
  • Refunds, disputes and chargebacks are between you, your customer and Stripe. A platform fee on a payment you later refund is not returned unless Stripe returns it.
  • If your shop adds a surcharge to card payments, it is your job to make sure that is allowed where you work and that you tell your customers as the rules require.

  • You give us permission to store and process those records only to run Bittings for you, to keep it secure, and to fix problems.
  • You can export your inventory, your sales and your documents while your account is open.
  • You are responsible for having the right to collect what you put in — for example a customer's driver's licence photo — and for telling your customers how your shop uses their information.
  • We keep backups, but no system is perfect. Keep your own copies of anything you could not afford to lose, including paperwork you are required to keep.

6. Key, vehicle and programming information

Bittings shows reference information: which keys fit which vehicles, keyways, chip types, FCC IDs, programming methods, VIN decoding and links to outside tools and suppliers. It comes from many sources, including information your own shop enters, and it can be wrong or out of date.

  • Check the information against the vehicle and the part in your hand before you cut, program or sell.
  • We are not responsible for a wrong key, a damaged part or module, lost time, or a lost job that comes from relying on reference information.
  • Links to outside sites are a convenience. We do not run those sites and are not responsible for them.

7. Your responsibilities as a locksmith

  • Hold whatever licences, registrations and insurance your state and city require, and follow the law in the work you do.
  • You must confirm that the person asking for a key has the right to it. Bittings helps you record identification and proof of ownership; it does not check them.
  • If you use the NASTF tools, your NASTF agreement and its deadlines are yours to meet. Bittings helps you gather and keep the paperwork. It does not file anything with NASTF for you, and it is not connected to, approved by or endorsed by NASTF.
  • Sales tax settings, tax-exempt sales and the records behind them are your responsibility. Bittings is not tax, legal or accounting advice.
  • Hours, commission and pay figures are worked out from what your shop enters. Check them before you pay anyone. Bittings is not a payroll service and does not file payroll taxes.

Cancellation & refunds

Last updated November 12, 2026

The short version. No contract and no cancellation fee. Email us to cancel, you keep access to the end of the month you have paid for, and you can take your records with you.

How to cancel

The account owner emails support@bittings.app from the email address they sign in with and says they want to cancel. We confirm by email, normally within one business day. Your cancellation counts from the day you sent it, not the day we replied.

When it takes effect

  • Your plan runs to the end of the month you have already paid for, and you are not billed again.
  • You keep full access until then.

Refunds

  • First payment: if Bittings is not right for your shop, tell us within 30 days of your first payment and we will refund that payment in full.
  • After that: we do not refund part of a month. Cancelling stops the next bill.
  • Our mistake: if we bill you twice or bill the wrong amount, tell us and we will put it right.
  • Refunds go back to the card or account that paid, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
  • Fees on card payments from your customers (Stripe's fees, and the 1% on the Starter plan) are charged per payment and are not refunded when you cancel.

Changing plan

  • Moving up: starts straight away. We bill the difference for the rest of the month.
  • Moving down, or removing users: starts with your next bill. Features that are not in the lower plan stop then; the records behind them are kept.

Your records after you cancel

WhenWhat happens
Before your plan endsExport your inventory, sales and documents. Download any D1 photos and receipts you are required to keep.
For 30 days after it endsThe account is read-only: the owner can sign in, look things up and export, but not add or change anything. Come back in this time and everything is as you left it.
Within 90 days after thatWe delete your shop's records from Bittings. Copies in our backups are overwritten as the backups roll over.

Want it gone sooner? The account owner can ask us by email to delete everything early. We keep our own billing records for as long as tax law requires. Your Stripe account, and the payment history in it, is yours and is not affected.

When we suspend or close an account

  • Unpaid bill: after 14 days unpaid we may make the account read-only. After 45 days unpaid we may close it, and the record timings above then apply.
  • Breaking the terms: we will normally tell you and give you a chance to fix it. If it is serious — fraud, breaking the law, or putting other shops' records at risk — we may suspend at once.
  • If we close Bittings or stop offering your plan, we will give you at least 60 days' notice, refund anything you have paid for time after the closing date, and keep exports available until then.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

The short version. We use information to run Bittings for your shop and nothing else. We do not sell it, we do not show ads, and there are no advertising trackers in the app or on this site.

Who this covers

This policy covers Bittings — the app at bittings.app and this website, getbittings.com — run by Turbo Keysmith LLC ("Bittings", "we"). There are two kinds of people it matters to:

  • Shops and their staff — the people who sign in to Bittings.
  • A shop's own customers — people whose details a shop records in Bittings. That information belongs to the shop, and we hold it only on the shop's behalf. If you are a customer of a locksmith who uses Bittings, please contact that locksmith about your information; we will help them answer you.
