02 · Stock

Key inventory.

Know what is on the shelf and what is in each van. Book in deliveries, count stock and see what to reorder.

What you can do.

  • Counts for the shop and each van.See what you have and where it is.
  • Categories.Keys (Smart Key, Flip, Transponder and more) and Accessories, each in its own place.
  • Low-stock and out-of-stock flags.The things you are running short on stand out.
  • Stock value.See the value of your stock at retail and at cost.
  • Receive and count.Receive deliveries and count stock to reconcile. A technician can be given a "receive" task to add new keys with price, cost and supplier link.
  • Automatic prices.Sale prices come from cost, with separate markups for smart keys and other keys. One tap reprices everything, and you can undo it.
  • Import and reorder.Import from a spreadsheet. The reorder list is grouped by supplier.
  • Scan a barcode.Scan a key’s barcode to find it.
