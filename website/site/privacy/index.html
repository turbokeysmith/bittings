Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Who We Are

Turbo Keysmith is a licensed mobile locksmith (Oklahoma #AC441081) based at 4201 N MacArthur Blvd, Warr Acres, OK 73122. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and in the course of providing locksmith service, and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

Service requests: when you call, text, WhatsApp, or use our contact form, we receive the details you share — typically your name, phone number, service address, and information about your vehicle or lock (for example year/make/model or VIN) needed to do the job.

Receipts & warranty records: we keep records of completed jobs (what was done, for whom, and where) so we can honor our 6-month key warranty and meet licensing and tax obligations.

Job applications: if you apply through our Careers page, we receive the information you submit in the application.

Payments: card and financing payments are processed by third-party payment providers. We never see or store your full card number.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to you, provide and schedule locksmith service, verify ownership or authorization where required by law, issue receipts, honor warranties, and meet legal, licensing, and tax requirements. We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

4. When We Share Information

We share information only with service providers that help us operate — our website hosting, the form service that delivers contact and careers submissions to us, and payment processors — and when the law requires it (for example, locksmith licensing or law-enforcement requests). These providers may only use your information to provide their service to us.

5. Cookies & Website Data

Our website doesn't use advertising cookies. It may store small preferences on your device (such as your language choice) and loads fonts and embedded content (such as Google reviews or maps) from third parties, which may receive standard technical data like your IP address when your browser loads them.

6. How Long We Keep Information

We keep job and receipt records as long as needed for warranty coverage and the retention periods required by Oklahoma licensing and tax law, then dispose of them. Contact messages that don't become jobs are kept only as long as useful for responding to you.

7. Your Choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (we may need to keep what the law requires us to keep). Call or text 405-870-5397 or email turbokeysmith@gmail.com.

8. Children

Our services and website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date.
