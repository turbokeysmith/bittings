Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Turbo Keysmith’s mobile locksmith service in the OKC metro.

Are you a mobile locksmith?

Yes. Turbo Keysmith is fully mobile — we come to you anywhere in the Oklahoma City metro, whether you're at home, at work, or stranded in a parking lot.

How fast can you get to me?

We book up fast — but we're usually very quick to reach you. And on the rare day there's a wait, you'll find we're worth it: licensed, dealer-level work done right the first time, not a rushed job. Call or text (405) 870-5397 and we'll give you a real ETA for your location before we head out.

How much will it cost?

You'll get a straight, flat-rate price before any work starts — we quote you on the phone and that's what you pay. No "$40 service call" bait, and no surprise fees when we arrive. Every vehicle and job is a little different, so call or text (405) 870-5397 for your exact price up front.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Oklahoma City, Edmond, Moore, Norman, Yukon, Warr Acres, Bethany, Midwest City, Del City, and the surrounding metro.

What are your hours?

We're open 24 hours a day, Monday through Saturday — and we stay open until 5:00 AM Sunday for the bar-and-club crowd heading home. We're then closed for the rest of Sunday and reopen Monday at 12:00 AM, running 24/6 all week. Call or text (405) 870-5397 anytime we're open.

Can you make a car key if I lost all of mine?

Yes. As a NASTF-authorized locksmith with dealer-level tools, we can cut and program replacement keys and fobs for most makes and models on-site — usually faster and for less than the dealership.

Does my insurance or roadside plan cover this?

Often, yes. Many roadside and insurance plans reimburse locksmith work — whether their dispatcher sent someone or you called us yourself. We give you an itemized invoice to submit to your provider, and calling us directly is usually faster than waiting on their dispatch. Check your specific policy for exact reimbursement terms.

How can I pay?

We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, and fast digital options like Amazon Pay, Cash App Pay, and Link — all through our secure online checkout on the Pay Now page. Need to spread the cost? We offer interest-free payment plans too — see the next question.

Do you offer financing or payment plans?

Yes. Quality locksmith work shouldn't have to wait for payday. On qualifying jobs you can split your bill into 4 interest-free payments with Klarna or Afterpay right in our secure checkout — approval takes seconds. PayPal Pay-in-4 is available on request. It's how we make dealer-level, NASTF-certified work affordable: we're not the cheapest locksmith, we're the best value, with the flexibility to pay over time. See payment options →

Do you warranty your car keys?

Yes — every automotive key and remote we program carries a 6-month workmanship warranty covering programming, remote/push-to-start function, and the cut. It doesn't cover lost, stolen, or damaged keys, dead fob batteries, or vehicle-side problems. If your car is drivable, bring it to us and a covered defect is fixed free; if it can't be driven, we'll come to you within our OKC service area. See our Warranty page for full terms.

Are you licensed?

Yes — Turbo Keysmith is a licensed Oklahoma locksmith, OK Lic. #AC441081.

How do I know you're not one of those "shady" locksmiths?

Fair question — the trade has earned a bad reputation from out-of-state call centers that lure you in with a cheap "$40 service call," subcontract your job to whoever's nearest, then triple the price once they're at your door. We're the opposite, on purpose. Turbo Keysmith is a licensed Oklahoma locksmith (OK Lic. #AC441081) with a real, verifiable shop at 4201 N MacArthur Blvd in Warr Acres — owned by an Oklahoman who has lived here for the past 33 years, not a faceless dispatch number. Our pricing is simple and told to you up front: a straight, flat-rate price before any work starts — no bait-and-switch and no surprise "extra" fees. We're also NASTF certified — the auto industry's federally recognized credentialing program for automotive locksmiths — which means we're vetted, background-checked and regulated, and every vehicle we make keys for has our Vehicle Security Professional (VSP) credentials logged against its VIN. In plain terms: there's a permanent, accountable federal record tying us to every car we touch — something a shady operator can't and won't do. Your car keys and remotes also carry a written 6-month warranty. Our advice for anyone hiring a locksmith: if they won't show a license, won't give a real local address, or change the price once they arrive — walk away. That's exactly what we will never do.
